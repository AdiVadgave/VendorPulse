[Heading 1] Objective:
[Normal] Shell currently runs vendor governance cycles (QBRs / EGBs) as a largely manual exercise, coordinators chase availability over email, scorecards arrive piecemeal across inboxes, alignment meetings recap the same recurring issues, and post-meeting minutes are inconsistent quarter on quarter. ZenVendorPulse is an agentic AI platform that automates the orchestration end-to-end while keeping every outbound action (emails, invites, briefs, minutes) under human approval. The proof-of-concept already demonstrates the six-stage workflow against a Gmail/Google-Forms-based mock environment. This document set scopes the work to lift that POC into a Shell-grade production deployment hosted in Shell's Azure tenant, integrated with Outlook + Teams, and removed entirely from Google's stack.
[Heading 1] 1. Scope/Capabilities Description
[Normal] Deploy the already developed POC onto Shell's tenant delivering six vendor-governance capabilities:
--- TABLE ---
Capability | What it does
A: Meeting Scheduling & Coordination | Proposes and books EGB/QBR meetings via Graph, coordinating Shell and vendor attendees through the service mailbox.
B: Scorecard Input & Validation | Collects and validates 1-5 ratings across 4 categories / 16 parameters: Risk & Compliance, Performance, Commercial, Relationship.
C: Internal Alignment Calls Support | Prepares Shell-side alignment materials, consolidating scorecards and surfacing discussion points ahead of vendor engagement.
D: Vendor Prep Call Support | Drafts vendor pushbacks in three stances (Factual, Neutral, Escalation) under a legal-review guardrail.
E: EGB/QBR Combined Meeting Support | Runs the combined governance meeting, capturing decisions, actions and minutes for human approval.
F: Cross-Cycle Memory & Trend Analysis | Retains prior-cycle data to track vendor performance trends and recurring themes across governance cycles.
--- END TABLE ---
